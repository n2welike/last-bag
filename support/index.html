Support

Found a bug, stuck on a champion, or have an idea? Write to us and we’ll reply as soon as we can.

Email: n2we.nlike@gmail.com

Please tell us your device model, iOS version and the hunt and fight number. A screenshot helps a lot.

Common questions

Do I need the internet? No. The whole game works offline.

What does the Full Game include? Two more champion hunts, Tempo and Endurance, with new items. It is a one-time purchase.

I bought the Full Game on another device. Tap a Tempo or Endurance hunt in Camp, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account. You will not be charged again.

Will my progress move to a new phone? Not yet: progress is saved only on the device where you play.

Are there ads? No.
