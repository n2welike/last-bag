Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

LAST BAG: Iron Hunt (“the app”) is an offline single-player game.

Data we collect: none

The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs that collect data.

Data on your device

Game progress and settings are saved only on your device, inside the app’s storage. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app only checks with the App Store whether the Full Game has been purchased on your Apple Account.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
